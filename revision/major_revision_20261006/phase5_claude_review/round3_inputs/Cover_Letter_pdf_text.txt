Dear Editor,
Re: Major revision of “UsherPipe: a genome-wide multi-evidence pipeline for prioritizing candidate genes in 
Usher syndrome and related ciliopathies”
Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript. We have addressed both reports in the accompanying 
point-by-point response and revised the manuscript and Supplementary Methods.
The revision distinguishes seed-free raw scoring from the control-calibrated HIGH priority tier and reports 
final control retention and filtering losses. It adds matched disease comparators, simple weighting baselines, 
shortlist-membership and missingness diagnostics, and analyses of the cerebellar expression proxy. We have 
retained the biological rationale while clarifying the score’s limited disease specificity and the absence of 
production hair-cell measurements.
We also completed a retrospective temporal evaluation of two historical analogues. The AI-assisted literature-
screened cohort is provisional pending individual human clinical/date verification. Forty-one of its 45 later 
target-association genes were in the historical protein-coding universe; neither analogue recovered a principal 
case in the top 100 or HIGH tier. Top-100 nonrecovery is reported in the Abstract; the main Results also show 
the empty or near-empty genome-wide historical HIGH tiers. Source substitutions, post-cutoff method 
development and non-exhaustive clinical ascertainment limit interpretation. The revised claim is transparent 
hypothesis organization, without demonstrated reliable novel disease-gene prediction. Production weights, 
scores and thresholds were not optimized against these outcomes.
The revised submission includes editable documents, figures, additional files, derived replay inputs and 
temporal protocols/results. Checksums identify the actual delivered artifacts. The original production run and 
submitted materials are preserved. Code and analysis records are available at 
https://github.com/gbanyan/usher-exploring/tree/major-revision-author-review-20261007; the completed 
numerical-analysis checkpoint is 736e16f, and the package manifest records the revised source and artifact 
hashes.
Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.
