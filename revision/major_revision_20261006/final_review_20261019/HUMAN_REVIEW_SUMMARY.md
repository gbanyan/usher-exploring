# Subsequent human author review

19 October 2026. This summary accompanies the unchanged frozen temporal analysis. References [57–64] are in the revised manuscript; identifiers for later reports remain in the frozen clinical ledger.


A human author examined primary reports for all 45 cases after the frozen temporal analysis, including reported family/pedigree information, phenotype, inheritance, functional mechanisms and publication dates. The review distinguished the number of individuals from unrelated families and separated the target phenotype from earlier disorders associated with the same gene. It was a source and interpretation check, not a new blinded prospective benchmark.

Three reports prompted particular family-evidence concerns. The initial *PDIA6* paper described one affected individual from one family [57]. The earlier *VWA8* developmental-disorder paper described nine individuals from one family [58], whereas the temporal target was a later retinal association. For *PLCG1*, a single-family immune-disorder report [59] was distinguished from a later multisystem report with auditory/ocular involvement and four families [60]. The frozen criterion requires at least two unrelated target-phenotype families cumulatively across eligible reports, not necessarily within each paper or displayed pedigree. The frozen screening ledger also cites later PDIA6 reports (PMIDs 35856135, 40974269), recording three families overall and a conservative lower bound of two target families; the VWA8 retinal reports (PMIDs 37012052, 40638000) give a target-family lower bound of two, and PLCG1 has a conservative target-family lower bound of three. These are the retained screening classifications, not new human inclusion decisions. These observations therefore require gene–target-specific interpretation; they do not by themselves constitute three exclusions. Eligibility of the flagged cases remains under review. No new human-approved cohort, revised denominator or recovery estimate is asserted.

The review also distinguished *CRLS1* mitochondrial cardiolipin deficiency with sensory manifestations [61], *CTNND1* retinal vascular disease [62], *DHRSX* dolichol-biosynthesis/glycosylation disease [63], and spliceosomal RNA-associated retinal disease involving *RNU4-2* and RNU6 genes [64]. Sensory manifestations alone do not establish a ciliary mechanism. The temporal scope intentionally includes inherited retinal/optic disease, hearing impairment and explicitly supported ciliopathies, rather than only mechanistically established ciliopathies.

Publication chronology was checked against the supplied reports and bibliographic records, distinguishing first public target evidence, preprints/early-online versions, acceptance and issue dates. Eligibility uses a supported first-publicity interval wholly after 31 December 2020; an exact day is unnecessary when the interval does not cross the cutoff. Most discrepancies concerned publication stages. For example, TMEM72 conference/abstract evidence in 2021–2022 predates the full paper's 2025 online/2026 issue dates. Exact earliest-day uncertainty remains documented for ATP11A, CREB3, MRPL49 and TMEM72. No newly confirmed pre-cutoff target association was found in this bounded audit. The original dated roster and numerical results remain preserved. Additional file 12 includes this review summary alongside the frozen analysis records; the complete working author-review form is not an additional submission attachment.
