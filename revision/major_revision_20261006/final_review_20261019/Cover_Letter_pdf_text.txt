Dear Editor,
Re: Major revision of “UsherPipe: a genome-wide multi-evidence pipeline for prioritizing candidate genes in 
Usher syndrome and related ciliopathies”
Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript. For submission 97b94353-679a-4ef3-a386-
69332e413872, we have addressed the editor and all three reviewers in the accompanying point-by-point 
response and revised the manuscript and Supplementary Methods.
The revision distinguishes seed-free raw scoring from the control-calibrated HIGH priority tier and reports 
final control retention and filtering losses. It adds matched disease comparators, simple weighting baselines, 
shortlist-membership and missingness diagnostics, and analyses of the cerebellar expression proxy. We have 
retained the biological rationale while clarifying the score’s limited disease specificity and the absence of 
production hair-cell measurements.
We also completed a retrospective temporal evaluation of two historical analogues. Subsequent human author 
review examined the primary reports and family/pedigree evidence for all 45 AI-screened cases, clarified 
publication versions and disease mechanisms, and identified target-family evidence concerns that are explicitly 
retained in the revised text. Forty-one of its 45 later target-association genes were in the historical protein-
coding universe; neither analogue recovered a principal case in the top 100 or HIGH tier. Top-100 nonrecovery 
is reported in the Abstract; the main Results also show the empty or near-empty genome-wide historical HIGH 
tiers. Source substitutions, post-cutoff method development and non-exhaustive clinical ascertainment limit 
interpretation. The revised claim is transparent hypothesis organization, without demonstrated reliable novel 
disease-gene prediction. Production weights, scores and thresholds were not optimized against these outcomes.
The revision also includes a contemporary GO functional description of the temporal cases; no enrichment 
passed the specified FDR threshold. This descriptive addition does not alter historical scores. The revised 
submission includes editable documents with embedded figures and legends, separate figure files, additional 
files, derived replay inputs and temporal protocols/results. We will check figure visibility in the journal-
generated review PDF before completing resubmission. Checksums identify the actual delivered artifacts. The 
original production run and submitted materials are preserved. Code and analysis records are available at 
https://github.com/gbanyan/usher-exploring/tree/major-revision-author-review-20261019; the preserved 7 
October author-review release remains separately identified, and the package manifest records the exact 
revised source and artifact hashes. The completed temporal numerical-analysis checkpoint is 736e16f.
Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.
