Dear Editor,
Re: Major revision of “UsherPipe: a genome-wide multi-evidence pipeline for prioritizing candidate genes in 
Usher syndrome and related ciliopathies”
Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript. For submission 97b94353-679a-4ef3-a386-
69332e413872, we have addressed the editor and all three reviewers in the accompanying point-by-point 
response and revised the manuscript and Supplementary Methods.
The revision distinguishes raw scoring from the control-calibrated HIGH priority tier and reports final control 
retention. We added matched disease comparators, simple scoring baselines, weight and missingness analyses, 
and an assessment of the cerebellar expression proxy. We also shortened the biological background, as 
suggested by Reviewer 1.
A retrospective temporal assessment tested two historical analogues. Neither recovered a principal case in the 
top 100 or HIGH (0/41 coding cases; 0/45 overall). Subsequent human review covered the primary reports and 
family/pedigree evidence for all 45 cases; unresolved target-family concerns are disclosed. We report the weak 
recovery and source limitations directly and restrict the conclusions to hypothesis generation. Production 
weights, scores and thresholds remain unchanged.
The revised files include embedded figures and legends, separate figure files, replay inputs and the temporal 
results. A separate contemporary GO description found no FDR-significant enrichment. We will check figure 
visibility in the journal-generated review PDF before completing resubmission. The fixed code and analysis 
release is https://github.com/gbanyan/usher-exploring/tree/major-revision-author-review-20261020; the 
package manifest records the delivered-file hashes.
Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.
