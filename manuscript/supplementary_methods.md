# Supplementary Methods and Analyses

## S1. Optional post-hoc shortlist refinement

The production scoring and tiering procedure starts from 20,116 Ensembl 113 protein-coding IDs, yields 20,081 analysis labels after 35 duplicate-symbol consolidations (including 573 unresolved ENSG fallback labels), and ends at the 62-gene HIGH tier. Remaining names derive from the Ensembl GTF `gene_name` field or an exact-ID legacy-cache fallback; these sources do not validate current canonical HGNC symbols. Table S1 contains the 35 merged-symbol mappings (34 MANE Select mappings and one fallback); Table S2 contains 1,694 excluded labels (1,666 below the LOW threshold and 28 with NULL composite scores). The analyses below are optional, post-hoc annotations evaluated within HIGH and do not alter composite scores, priority tiers, or the six production evidence layers.

### S1.1 Tau tissue-specificity thresholds

Tau specificity was propagated from `tissue_expression` into the scored-gene output and evaluated only as an exploratory annotation after production tier assignment. The final HIGH tier contains 62 genes. Threshold-specific Tau counts are reported in the generated expression-shortlist artifacts rather than treated as a production result. Duplicate tissue-expression records are aggregated before thresholding, and missing Tau is retained as missing.

### S1.2 GSE135913 fetal cochlear data

Processed cluster-level matrices from GEO series GSE135913 [41] were used in local-cache-only mode for human cochlea samples GSM4037819 (15 weeks), GSM4037820 (17 weeks), and GSM4037821 (23 weeks). The local input relative paths and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `data/report/exploration/gse135913_hair_cell_provenance.json`; this manifest contains no source URLs and should not be read as complete source-level provenance or proof of immutable inputs for the broader production run. The aggregate output is restricted to 15,608 of the 20,081 current analysis labels.

The matrices provide standardized CellFindR mean expression by cluster but do not include cell-type labels. Putative hair-cell clusters were therefore identified with a prespecified panel that excludes all Usher and SYSCILIA positive-control genes: *ATOH1, OTOF, POU4F3, GFI1, RBM24, LMO7, EPS8L2,* and *SLC17A8*. A sample was accepted only when at least four markers were represented, the highest aggregate marker mean was positive, and its margin over the next-ranked cluster was at least 0.5. The 15-week sample selected cluster 11 (8/8 markers; margin 4.735) and the 17-week sample selected cluster 10 (8/8; margin 5.221). The 23-week sample was excluded: five markers were represented, but the margin between the first and second clusters was only 0.097.

To test marker dependence, cluster selection was repeated after removing each marker individually. Every leave-one-marker-out run selected cluster 11 at 15 weeks and cluster 10 at 17 weeks. Because the primary marker panel contains neither *MYO7A* nor *PCDH15*, retention of these or other Usher controls is not used to define the clusters. In the final HIGH tier, 61 of 62 genes had a non-NULL GSE135913 aggregate; this exploratory resource is not part of the production expression layer.

Per-gene standardized means from accepted clusters were averaged across samples with NULL preservation. The output also records contributing sample count and a developmental-consistency measure. In the final HIGH tier, the fetal hair-cell Q75 strategy retained 16 genes and retained two of the two established Usher controls present in HIGH, but only two of the three total positive controls present in HIGH. Hair-cell Q75 denotes the 75th percentile calculated among genes already assigned to HIGH; it is a relative experimental-priority annotation, not a claim of absent expression below the threshold.

### S1.3 Interpretation

Tau, hair-cell Q75, photoreceptor Q75, HPA-retina concordance, and the hair-cell/photoreceptor intersection represent alternative biological assumptions. In the final rerun, photoreceptor Q75 retained 16 genes, the hair-cell/photoreceptor intersection retained five, and HPA-retina concordance was NA/not evaluable because the local HPA-retina threshold was unavailable. Direct protein evidence retained 23 genes, the expression-OR-protein strategy retained 34, and the expression-AND-protein strategy retained five. They are not a sequential filtering cascade. Missing or below-threshold expression does not change the production tier and is not treated as evidence against disease relevance.

## S2. Data-driven weight fitting

As post-hoc internal robustness analyses, we evaluated cross-validated grid search over the weight simplex and L2-penalized logistic regression using the selected disease controls. The grid search evaluated 8,856 candidate weight vectors. Logistic regression produced clipped-and-normalized weights `[0, 0, 0, 0.032, 0.903, 0.065]` for gnomAD, expression, annotation, localization, animal model, and literature, respectively. It changed the known-control median from 92.8% to 98.7% and the housekeeping median from 94.8% to 75.1%. The default raw composite ≥0.70 housekeeping count was 2, whereas the logistic-regression count was 0; the default gated HIGH count was already 0. The default-versus-logistic-regression full-ranking Spearman correlation was 0.646. In a five-fold positive-label-held-out internal cross-validation summary, the held-out positive median was 98.2%; the matched negatives were constructed globally rather than held out within each fold, so leakage is possible and this is not independent held-out validation. These analyses concentrated weight on the animal-model layer and substantially altered the genome-wide ranking. Because the same limited control set would influence model selection and evaluation, they were treated as tests of possible over-specialization, not as independent validation or a basis for replacing the biologically specified production weights. The production sensitivity analysis used absolute ±0.05 and ±0.10 weight-point perturbations: each raw delta was applied to one weight and then all six weights were renormalized to sum to one, so the final change is not literally the raw delta and the other weights also change. Sixteen of 24 met ρ ≥ 0.85, with mean ρ = 0.8548 and range 0.6051–0.9770. Implementations and detailed outputs are provided by `scripts/weight_tuning.py` and `scripts/weight_logreg.py`.


## S3. Implemented evidence semantics and source coverage

The raw composite is the sum of observed weighted layer values divided by the sum of observed weights. Weights are constraint/expression 0.20 each and annotation/localization/animal/literature 0.15 each. These are heuristic priorities, not fitted probabilities. An observed zero participates in the denominator; NULL does not. The legacy output column `confidence_tier` is retained for schema compatibility but denotes a priority tier. HIGH requires score ≥0.70, at least three observed layers and the source-level gate described in the main Methods. MEDIUM requires score ≥0.40 and at least two layers; LOW requires score ≥0.20. The gate is not applied to raw ranks.

### S3.1 Expression

Within each quantitative source, target means (retina/cerebellum) are divided by background means (testis/fallopian tube) only when both exist and the background is positive. HPA quantitative measurements take precedence over the ordinal HPA fallback when target and background columns are available in the source table. For ordinal HPA fallback, categories Not detected/Low/Medium/High are coded 0/1/2/3, then normalized within tissue using average ranks among positive observations; zero stays zero and NULL stays NULL. Target/background contrast is computed on these tissue percentiles, never on the raw ordinal codes. Available HPA and GTEx contrasts are averaged. Their resulting positive contrast values are percentile-normalized before combination.

Quantitative restricted-panel Tau is Σ(1−xᵢ/max(x))/(n−1), requiring at least two observed tissues and a positive maximum, calculated separately for HPA nTPM and GTEx TPM. Available source Taus are averaged. This is specificity within the four-tissue panel, not whole-body specificity. Census photoreceptor expression is an independently ranked target feature. Quantitative/ordinal target values are normalized within their respective source columns, with positive average ranks divided by the positive-observation count and observed zeros retained as zero. The maximum available target percentile supplies the third component. Expression combines contrast percentile (0.4), source Tau (0.3) and maximum target percentile (0.3), renormalizing over non-NULL components. A gene may therefore receive partial expression evidence from background-only quantitative Tau even with no measured retinal target. No hair-cell component enters production scoring.

The stored 20,116-ID table has 0 HPA quantitative-retina observations, 0 GTEx-retina observations, 112 retinal HPA protein observations and 20,078 non-NULL Census photoreceptor values including zeros. The production proxy-removal sensitivity removes cerebellum columns and recomputes source transforms and genome-wide ranks rather than simply subtracting a component. It retains the original remaining measurements and weights. Per-control source values, expression contribution and resulting rank/tier changes are in the Phase 2 expression audit.

### S3.2 Annotation, localization, animal and literature

Annotation uses 0.5 × log₂(GO count+1)/maximum log₂(GO count+1), 0.3 × UniProt score/5 and 0.2 × binary pathway membership. Missing subcomponents contribute zero; all three missing yields NULL. This differs from the between-layer available-weight denominator.

Localization proximity checks direct compartment flags first (1.0), recognized adjacent compartments second (0.5), embedded compendium fallback third (0.3), then observed non-ciliary location (0). Adjacent strings include cytoskeleton, microtubule, cell junction and focal adhesion. HPA reliability factors are Enhanced/Supported=1.0 and Approved/Uncertain=0.6; all are antibody-staining modalities. If HPA reliability is unavailable, curated compendium evidence has factor 0.5. Mixed evidence uses the HPA factor rather than adding two scores. No usable modality yields NULL. Embedded lists have no validated historical snapshot date and are not an acquired complete experimental/proteomics atlas. The HIGH gate ORs the five direct compartment flags and two compendium flags, or animal score ≥0.12765713253595054. A positive adjacent localization score alone is insufficient. `has_cilia_signal` instead records positive aggregate localization or animal evidence and is not equivalent to gate passage.

Animal evidence unions distinct MGI/IMPC mouse term names and adds the zebrafish count to obtain a combined sensory count. The base score is 0.4 × mouse phenotype-presence flag × mouse HCOP confidence + 0.3 × zebrafish phenotype-presence flag × zebrafish confidence (HIGH=1.0, MEDIUM=0.7, LOW=0.4). It is multiplied by log₂(combined sensory count+1)/log₂(global maximum combined count+1), then clipped to [0,1]; counts are not separately log-normalized per species. The absence of an ortholog is NULL; an observed mapping without recorded sensory phenotype can be zero. Human curation and animal experiments may share published knowledge.

Literature context counts have weights cilia=2, sensory=2, cytoskeleton=1 and polarity=1; missing contextual subcounts contribute zero. Implemented classification checks direct experimental first, then high-throughput, functional, incidental and none, with factors 1.0/0.3/0.6/0.1/0.0. Direct classification requires one shared PMID in the gene, experimental and cilia/sensory context sets. The quality-weighted count is divided by log₂(total gene-linked publications+1). Positive raw values receive their average rank among all non-NULL raw values divided by the non-NULL count; nonpositive values are zero and missing raw scores remain NULL. This does not fully correct ascertainment, nor does “direct” establish clinical causality.

## S4. Control traces, completeness, correlations and ranking sensitivities

Phase 2 was specified before the added outcomes. Its complete tables are in `revision/major_revision_20261006/results/`, with definitions in `analysis_spec.md` and summaries in `phase2_report.md`. The nine established Usher and 28 selected SYSCILIA controls are distinct evaluation groups, not a random validation cohort. Percentiles use (minimum tied rank−1)/(n−1) among non-NULL scores; top-K positions break ties by retained Ensembl ID.

All 62 HIGH labels have five or six layers (33 and 29), with zero supported by only three or four. Raising the minimum to four/five changes none; six retains 29. Eight raw top-100 labels have only one layer, all classified LOW. Layer count correlates with composite score at ρ=0.2401835 (n=20,053). Pairwise layer correlations use pair-specific observed genes and retain zeros. Annotation–literature is the largest (ρ=0.370354; n=19,114); correlations are descriptive and do not demonstrate independent information sources.

Each of 24 absolute ±0.05/±0.10 perturbations changes one raw weight, then renormalizes all weights to one. Together with equal weights these form a finite 25-configuration family. Membership is reported for every gene in the default top 25/50/100, including unstable candidates; 8/17/41 survive every configuration. Whole-universe correlation and exact shared top-K counts are reported alongside the earlier conditional Spearman statistic on overlapping top-100 genes. The latter has mean 0.8548 and 16/24 above 0.85; it must not be described as full-ranking correlation. Each of six leave-one-layer-out analyses renormalizes the available remaining weights. Single-layer baselines use observed genes only, with their own denominators; no NULL-to-zero substitution is made. Default top100 recovery is Usher 2/SYSCILIA 1, compared with animal-only 4/11. The integrated score has not been shown to outperform the strongest layer for internal recovery.

## S5. Frozen disease-comparator analysis and review-prompted diagnostics

The comparator protocol was committed at `0ae2c3d`, and roster at `61abb61`, before score-outcome inspection. Sources were the exact 2026-10-06 ClinGen validity export, exact HGNC live export and HPO v2026-09-01; SHA-256, download completion times and URLs are in `phase3_roster/manifest.json`. The current revision distributes the two otherwise mutable live exports. Require Strong/Definitive validity, an Approved unambiguous HGNC Ensembl/Entrez mapping to the retained universe and HPO annotations. Exclude original controls and assertions/panels matching auditory, retinal or ciliary terms; exclude all descendants of the ten frozen HPO hearing, eye, motile-cilium, mucociliary and situs roots listed in the manifest. Reasons overlap and are not disjoint counts.

The eligibility pool contains 420 genes, of which 418 have non-NULL publication and GO counts. Matching log1p-transforms both raw counts and divides by their SD in the combined complete comparator/control population. Maximum-cardinality/minimum-squared-distance assignment supplies three unique matches per control, with no reuse and each absolute coordinate difference ≤0.75 SD (observed maximum 0.70945). All 37 controls matched: 27 Usher and 84 SYSCILIA comparators. Within-control averaging gives every control equal weight. Covariate SD differences after matching are publication/GO +0.0093/+0.0105 for Usher and −0.0412/+0.0385 for SYSCILIA. This balances two measured nuisance quantities rather than full research intensity or every layer.

Default median percentiles are Usher 97.77 versus matches 64.21, and SYSCILIA 91.93 versus matches 60.30. Default wins are 24/27 and 72/84; no pair is unranked. There are zero HIGH genes among the matches, but 110/111 already fail the raw threshold. The broader pool retains three HIGH genes, CRB2/ATP1A1/DYNC1H1. They were not removed after outcomes. A subsequent, non-systematic primary-source check found pre-existing sensory biology: CRB2 retinal degeneration (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6747345/), ATP1A1 cochlear barrier (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3031570/) and DYNC1H1 photoreceptors (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7951903/). These explain residual overlap rather than validate novel Usher associations. Neither the frozen pool nor its remaining genes are verified biological negatives.

An independent review prompted additional post-outcome diagnostics, specified in `phase4_spec.md`. No production score, threshold, matching caliper or roster changed. Common-observed-layer scores compare each pair using only shared non-NULL layers, retaining original weights over those layers; they are pair-dependent means, not a new genome-wide ranking. All six single omissions and joint animal/literature omission use the original 111 pairs without rematching. A tie contributes 0.5 and an unranked pair stays missing. Every pair was ranked and no tie occurred. Pair means and equally weighted control means agree because every control has three matches.

## S6. Restricted archival reconstruction and tissue audit

The cutoff protocol (`temporal_extension/protocol.md`, commit `377eae3`) preceded rank inspection. Three manually ascertained examples have later initial human associations located in the audit: CEP162 (2023), CFAP20 (initial candidate March 2022, subsequent independent November 2022 report) and LRRC45 (putative October 2021 report, later stronger evidence). Pre-cutoff ciliary biology was allowed; they are not a newly discovered Usher-positive cohort. Candidate and causal reports are distinguished. TMEM218 was excluded after primary full-text publication date 2020-11-21 contradicted apparent 2021 indexing. Other known pre-cutoff associations were excluded. Ascertainment was non-exhaustive.

The historical population uses Approved protein-coding HGNC entries in the 2020-10-01 archive with unique Ensembl mappings (19,167; two ambiguous rows excluded). Sources actually reconstructed were gnomAD 2.1.1 gene LOEUF, GO 2020-12-08 human GAF, HPA v20 normal-tissue ordinal protein and subcellular tables. Positive GO terms exclude NOT annotations and map through historical UniProt IDs, then unambiguous archived symbols. Annotation retains only its 0.5 GO component with missing UniProt/pathway subcomponents contributing zero, unlike wholly unavailable animal/literature layers omitted from the denominator. HPA ordinal data receive no quantitative Tau. Undated present-day compendia and modern identifiers/scores were not imported. Four layer weights remain 0.20/0.20/0.15/0.15, renormalized per observed gene. All-gene components, six ranking schemes and case traces are preserved; no historical HIGH tiers were assigned.

Of 19,033 scored genes, default ranks for CEP162/CFAP20/LRRC45 were 8,289/11,004/253. Equal-weight ranks were 8,460/11,327/161; no default case entered top100. LRRC45 localization was tied at the maximum with 77 genes; its favorable localization rank does not establish integrated success. All three lacked accepted retinal HPA observations. CEP162 protein categories were cerebellum/testis/fallopian 2/2/2; LRRC45 3/2/3; CFAP20 lacked all four panel observations. Ordinal categories are not quantitative fold changes.

Accepted HPA protein coverage in the historical population was retina 107 genes (all positive), cerebellum 10,894 (7,061 positive/3,833 observed zero), testis 10,917 (8,836/2,081), fallopian tube 10,898 (7,309/3,589). Top25/50/100 contained 10/11/11 one-layer genes. Historical top100 comprised 11 one-layer, ten two-layer, 67 three-layer and 12 four-layer genes; 83 lacked localization, 22 expression, 12 annotation and three constraint. Missingness counts overlap. The earlier independent-review shorthand “63 localization-missing” counted only the three-layer subgroup; the full count is 83.

This is an archive/scoring diagnostic, not full temporal validation. Historical MGI/MP, ZFIN and IMPC resources were partially accessible, but full production-compatible animal reconstruction including historical orthology confidence was not completed. An archived gene2pubmed transfer was incomplete, not an acquired full source. GTEx v8 and historical HPA RNA archives were available but outside the executed restricted source contract, not proven inaccessible. Modern Census data cannot be assumed available at the cutoff. Manual case selection, partial layers, changed measurement coverage and sparse means prevent estimation of novel-gene sensitivity or independent HIGH specificity.
